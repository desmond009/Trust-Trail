# Consent to take part: consent screen study (TrustTrail)

**Study:** How well do students understand what they share when they log in to university services?
**Team:** [names of the three team members], IIIT Dharwad
**Supervisor:** [name of the course instructor or supervisor]
**Contact:** [team email]

## What you will do

You will play an **invented student**, "Asha Kulkarni". You will see two kinds of "share your
details" screens from made-up university services, four short tasks in all. After each task we
ask a few questions about what you shared. It takes about **10 minutes**.

You will never enter your own name, email, roll number or password. The account you use is
a demo account we give you.

## What we record

- A participant code (for example P07), not your name
- What you chose on each screen, and how long it took
- Your answers to the questions, including any comment you choose to type

We do **not** record your name, roll number, email, IP address or anything you type outside
the study pages. Please don't type names or personal details into the comment box.

## Your choices

- Taking part is **voluntary**. Saying no, or stopping halfway, has no effect on your grades,
  your relationship with us, or anything else.
- You can stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can ask us to delete your answers until **[date, e.g. one week after the session]**.
  Tell us your participant code, which is on the slip we give you.
- Results appear only as group numbers (for example "12 of 16 participants…") in our course
  report and presentation. Comments may be quoted without your code.

## Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond a few minutes of your time. There is no payment. You help us
judge whether our design is understandable.
